🔬 Scientific provenance

How it works

Forecasts are checked against hourly PM2.5 readings from the OpenAQ network in Lahore. Where a reference monitor and low-cost sensors are at the same place, the reference monitor is used; otherwise the middle value of the corrected low-cost sensors. The city value is the average of 94 stations (1 reference, 93 low-cost).

SmogSense combines recent sensor readings, satellite fire detections and atmospheric model forecasts. A learning model, first trained on Delhi's dense monitoring network and then adapted to Lahore's few sensors, estimates a range of likely PM2.5 levels.
